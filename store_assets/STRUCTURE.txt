ParkingLink — Store Submission Assets
=====================================

Structure for Play Store + App Store metadata.

store_assets/
├── shared/
│   ├── privacy_url.txt          # Canonical privacy policy URL
│   ├── terms_url.txt
│   ├── description_vi.txt       # Full store description (Vietnamese)
│   ├── description_en.txt       # Full store description (English)
│   ├── short_description_vi.txt # Play Store short desc (≤80 chars)
│   └── release_notes_vi.txt     # What's new template
├── android/
│   ├── user/
│   │   ├── screenshots/         # Phone: 1080×1920 or 1080×2340 (min 2)
│   │   ├── feature_graphic/     # 1024×500 PNG
│   │   └── icon/              # 512×512 PNG (optional, launcher used)
│   └── staff/
│       ├── screenshots/
│       ├── feature_graphic/
│       └── icon/
└── ios/
    ├── user/
    │   ├── screenshots/         # 6.7" 1290×2796, 6.5" 1284×2778
    │   └── app_preview/         # Optional video
    └── staff/
        ├── screenshots/
        └── app_preview/

Upload targets:
- Google Play: 2 separate apps (com.parkinglink.user, com.parkinglink.staff)
- App Store Connect: 2 separate apps (com.parkinglink.user, com.parkinglink.staff)
